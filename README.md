# Shift

**Rescuing expiring AI compute on 0G.** Every night at 00:00 UTC, unused 0G compute credits expire. Shift spends them on your queued AI tasks with an optimizer powered by 0G decision models, donates what is left to public-good translations, and anchors who owns and who donated each result on 0G Chain.

> Built at ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026. Status: in progress — see [`PROGRESS.md`](PROGRESS.md).

## Why it needs 0G
Without 0G, there are no expiring daily credits to rescue, no TEE-verified receipts proving which model produced each result, and no on-chain record of who donated compute to the commons.
